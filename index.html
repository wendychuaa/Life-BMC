What does it mean to lead a Meaningful Life?

Day 1: 22 October 2026 - #lastsembestsem

With a full-time job lined up next August at J.P.Morgan, I’m already at the upper hand amongst my peers. I don’t have the anxiety of job searching, I don’t have to worry about my grades anymore, I don’t need to care too much. But with all the time in the world, why do I still feel like something is lacking? I don’t have anything planned for the next 10 months.

Should I be spending the time more wisely? Should I find another job, even though I don’t need to? Should I rest and lounge all day, where I fall asleep to Tik Toks in the background at 4am, then decide to get out of bed at 4pm only when I’ve reached a point where I think to myself, “What am I doing with my life?"

I’ve worked so hard for the past few years, everything was leading up to this moment where my future is more certain. But why am I still worried of falling behind? Nothing changes if nothing changes.

They say it takes 21 days to build a habit. I invite you, Prof Say, to see the steps I’m trying to take to improve my life in the next 21 days.

Day 21: 12 November 2026 - #lastsembestsem

In the past 3 weeks, I found myself consciously reminding myself of these micro-habits that I have set. From the plot above, I have accomplished my goal of waking up before 4pm everyday, with only one day that I woke up after. I try to put my phone away earlier so that I can wake up fresh. I also put in more effort into making my days more meaningful.

I feel that my condition has definitely gotten a bit better, even though I did not manage to accomplish every single goal every day. However, the feeling where I'm wasting my life away does come and go. It's definitely not something that I can get rid of in such a short period of time. It does make me hopeful, though, that you can restart your life whenever you want.

I realised that I got energy from having conversations with my friends, and when I'm alone, it's easier to get lost in my own thoughts. I also realised that I do like recording my life and having something to look back on! I plan to brush up on my editing skills over the next few months. This activity has made me more mindful of how I want to lead my life, and I'm so grateful that I have this channel to attempt a 'Reset'.

Where I am now (#feelunalive)

A Week with No Structure & Meaning :(

Monday to Wednesday are full: capstone, teaching assistant shifts, pilates, friends. Thursday to Sunday have no shape. I sleep until 4pm, scroll, and the day is gone before it starts. The parts of my life that work, reading and my friends, never get an anchor on those days.

The macro gap

Good foundations, uneven rhythm

My relationships and routines are healthy, but four days a week have no structure, so my sleep and energy cost me the days I’m trying to enjoy. My JPMorgan start date in August 2027 gives me about nine months to build a rhythm I want to carry into the job.

Where I want to be (#feelalive)

A Week with Structure & Meaning :)

1. Wake up before 12pm everyday
2. Inculcate a reading habit
3. Utilise my free time to build three skills that would help me later: finance knowledge, video editing, and coding.
